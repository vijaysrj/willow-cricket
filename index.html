How to play

Swipe toward where you want the ball to go. The moment your finger lifts is the moment you hit, so finish the swipe as the ball reaches the bat. Swipe up to hit straight, right for the off side, left for the leg side, and down to play behind the wicket.

Tap to defend. Do nothing to leave it, but anything on the stumps will knock them over.

Switch on Loft to hit in the air: more sixes, more catches. Play with the line, because hitting across a wide ball costs you timing, and don't try to loft a yorker.

On a keyboard: arrow keys to hit, Space to defend, L to toggle loft.
